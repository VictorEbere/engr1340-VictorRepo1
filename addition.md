# Addition of Two Integers

Addition is the process of combining two integer values to find their sum.

The addition operator in programming is `+`.

## Example 1

If we add 5 and 3:

5 + 3 = 8

Therefore, the sum is **8**.

## Example 2

If we add 10 and 7:

10 + 7 = 17

Therefore, the sum is **17**.
